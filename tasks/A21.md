## Implementation steps

1. Implement scheduled-arrival open-loop workload with separate dispatch/admission/completion times and generator saturation detection.
2. Keep failures/timeouts/rejections in datasets; preserve raw histograms/overflow and independent process repetitions.
3. Record exact hardware/ISA/power/OS/features/locks/artifact hashes, warm/cold cache/durability/TLS/serialization settings.
4. Collect managed/native allocations, copied bytes, owner/permit counts, RSS/private working set/thread stacks and engineering cost.
5. Predeclare workload cells and noise-based regression analysis; override BDN host-native ISA when testing portable releases.
