## 实施步骤

1. Exercise every D20 operation using independent durable-data and protocol oracles: operations/items/outbox atomicity, same/different hash replay, tenant/principal isolation, authorized recovery, keyset paging and attachment upload/download.
2. Inject DB faults before BEGIN/write/COMMIT dispatch and after dispatch or acknowledgement: lost COMMIT replies, failed transactions, early stream exit, server/process restart, SQLite busy/disk-full and pool reset failure. Reconcile transaction effect and same-session identity independently; preserve Unknown, rollback-unconfirmed and provider-cleanup distinctions.
3. Crash between outbox publish and acknowledgement recording, then prove repeated delivery attempts cause one deduplicated consumer effect. Crash attachment work before durable blob, after rename but before DB commit, and during orphan collection; verify no successful reference targets incomplete content and no active/recovery blob is collected. Cover actual Windows/Linux filesystem persistence differences without claiming power-loss guarantees from a process crash alone.
4. Test retention/cleanup against in-progress protected recovery and outbox references, expired-key semantics, cross-tenant same keys and tampered cursors. A single not-found read cannot authorize an unprotected business replay.
5. Inject slow upload/download, over-budget decompression/JSON/chunks, HTTP1 half-close/HTTP2 reset, handler faults, old permits, late cancellation and shutdown races. Test CloseAsync Pending ending response waiting while resources remain charged, followed by eventual safe DisposeAsync join; Body EOF or channel closed alone never proves byte-owner retirement.
6. Run repeated load/drain/idle cycles and D20's staged 30-minute steady, 24-hour regression and 72-hour release soak profiles with fixed workload and faults. Freeze load/duration/resource thresholds before the run, record live owners/roots/permits/threads/handles plus RSS trends, and keep missing release-soak rows unaccepted rather than substituting a shorter run.
7. Record exact source/native/package/AOT/DB/storage/profile identity, failure seed, timeline, raw observations and minimized reproductions. Functional oracle failure blocks performance/release claims; every executed or unexecuted profile is explicit.

## 错误、取消、内存与并发

- External timeout, process termination, Body EOF and an HTTP cancellation token are not graceful resource retirement or proof that a COMMIT rolled back.
- Flat RSS is not proof of no leak; leaked owners or still-live byte charges fail even if allocator retention hides growth. Conversely, owned-byte caps alone do not bound driver/protocol/TLS/GC/whole-process RSS.
- Outbox is at-least-once and blob-before-reference is not distributed atomic commit; preserve those explicit limits while independently checking actual business effects and reachable content.
- Pending cleanup must retain finite reserved capacity and ownership without deadlocking control paths or keeping an ended request DI scope alive. Primary errors and Unknown outcomes survive cleanup failures.
