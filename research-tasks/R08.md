Research questions:

- ForeignHandler registration generations, unregistration and body/context retirement; callbacks enter only a bounded dispatcher and do not execute arbitrary synchronous user code.
- Whether unregistration returning guarantees quiescence; whether Dispose inside a callback can deadlock; how to prevent foreign threads and late callbacks from accessing reclaimed contexts

Source mechanisms:

- callback registration
- signal dispatch
- GCHandle cleanup
- cancellation callbacks
